# 9. Playback, Audition and Export

> From [CucCut talking-head cut technical plan (prototype rounds 192–197 → kernel / serve / App v2 / Web)](../ccut-talking-head-cut-design.md)

## 9.1 Playback

Collapsing of cut segments is already handled by the existing `TimelineMap::playback_step / next_position`. When the playhead enters a cut segment, it jumps straight to the end of that segment; no changes are needed. Suggested segments do not affect playback (they are not in `cuts[]`).

## 9.2 Audition (C-R9)

Audition means listening to the stretch that is about to be cut, so it must play on the **source clock** and **must not collapse**. Both players get a one-shot mode `audition(source_start, source_end)`: seek to the start of `audition_window`, stop when the end point is reached, and during this time the transport timecode shows source time with an "Audition" badge. When it ends, the playhead returns to its timeline position from before audition started. Subtitle overlays during audition are projected by source time (same convention as `render_source_subtitles`), not through collapsing.

## 9.3 Export

`sources[].cuts[]` is already consumed by `timeline_media::build_plan`, `ccut-editable::build_main_segments` and the subtitle `clamp_source_event`. Suggestions are not exported; no changes. The `--no-cuts` escape hatch is unchanged. Hard-splicing audio at cut seams is covered in §11.
