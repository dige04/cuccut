# Gemini TTS voice reference

## Applicable models and how to choose

This page describes only Gemini TTS's preset voice system; see the [official Gemini TTS voice documentation](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/speech-generation#voice-options). What is actually available depends on the chosen model, its version and the capabilities the service exposes, so check with {{tool:models_capabilities}} first; this is not a voice list shared by all of CucCut's TTS models.

Intersect the voices the current service returns with this table, then filter by what the role needs. For example, narration can be filtered from Informative / Clear, a friendly voice-over from Warm / Friendly, and an upbeat character from Upbeat / Lively; the characteristics and Gender labels are for filtering, and the final choice is made by listening; they have not yet been auditioned one by one in CucCut. Do not lock in Charon just because speech-rate calibration used it.

Other TTS models use their own voice reference files and available voice IDs; the same name does not mean compatibility across services. Go back to the [voice guide](voice.md) for the general rules on synthesis, language and duration.
