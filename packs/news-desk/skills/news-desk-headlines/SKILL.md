# News desk headlines

A headline in this style says what happened, in plain words, in at most eight words. It names the actor and the action. It does not ask a question, tease, or use "shocking", "you won't believe" or any word that promises more than the story holds.

## Steps

1. Read the transcript of the take and find the sentence that says what happened.
2. Write five options of at most eight words each. Each one states a fact from the take; none adds a fact the take does not say.
3. Show the options to the user and wait for their pick. Do not choose for them.
4. Put the chosen headline on screen as the opening title for the first two seconds, in capitals, top left, over the first shot.

## Checks

- Every word of the headline can be traced to the take or to a source the user named.
- Names are spelled the way the glossary spells them.
