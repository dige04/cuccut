# News desk

Who these videos are for: people who follow the news on their phone and give a story about ten seconds before they decide to stay. They know the big names and none of the jargon.

What a good one is:

- One story per video. If the take wanders into a second story, cut the second one, however good it is.
- The first spoken line says what happened, in plain words. No greeting, no channel name, no "so today".
- Facts come in the order they happened. An opinion is said as an opinion, after the facts, and never on top of a quote.
- Every fact that came from somewhere else names its source on screen while it is said: a red lower third, in capitals, bottom left.
- Somebody else's clip runs whole or not at all. Never cut inside a quote to change what it says.
- Cut the stalls, the retakes and the filler words. Keep the breaths that make a sentence land.
- Captions follow every word, two lines at most, and never cover a lower third.
- The video ends on the last fact or the last sentence of the take, not on a sign-off.

How it sounds: calm and quick. Music only under the opening title, low, and out before the first fact.

Length: 45 to 90 seconds. If the take runs longer, it is two stories.
